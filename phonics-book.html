A NOTE FOR YOUR READING BUDDY

Two letters. One sound.

A digraph is two letters working together to make one sound. Read together, tap a word to hear it, then let your child try. Each friend has a meet, story, and practice page.

  • SH: a long, soft sound, as in ship.
  • CH: a short train sound, as in chip.
  • TH: put the tongue gently between the teeth and blow, as in thin. TH can also buzz, as in this; this book practices the quiet sound.
  • WH: as in whale. Many English speakers say it like W; some use a breathier sound. Both are welcome. We avoid who, which has a different sound.
  • CK: one quick /k/ sound, as in duck.

Audio reads whole words using your device’s English voice. It does not model isolated digraph sounds. Voice quality and availability depend on your browser and device.

Use the arrows, your keyboard’s ← and → keys, or swipe the picture to turn pages. Nothing is recorded or uploaded by this website. Your browser’s voice service may use a network connection.
